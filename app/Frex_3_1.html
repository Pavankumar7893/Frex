Example: a 100 MB source at 25% targets approximately 25 MB. Two-pass encoding estimates the required video bitrate; final size can vary slightly.

Two-pass encoding targets an average bitrate. Audio and container overhead are included in the bitrate estimate, so the final size is approximate—not byte-exact.

CRF is quality-based, not size-based. Start around 23–26 for H.264. Higher numbers compress more but lose more detail.
