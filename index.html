The toolkit

Made for practical workflows.

A focused desktop utility for downloading media and turning video into usable image sequences.

↓

Video downloader

Single downloads, sequential queues, quality and format choices, plus trim options where supported.

▦

Frame extraction

Choose a sampling rate, review paginated frame previews, select the frames you need, and export PNG or JPG ZIPs.

⌁

Local-first workflow

Media processing runs on your Windows PC. The app uses a local service rather than a hosted media-processing backend.
